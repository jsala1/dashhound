# Privacy

Full legal notice: **https://dashhoundrewind.com/legal**

In short — it keeps, it doesn't watch:

- **Nothing leaves your phone because of Dashhound.** No account, no server, no analytics, no tracking (the Meta SDK analytics are opted out).
- **Memory, not recording.** The last ~45 seconds live in RAM only. A clip is written to disk **only when you ask** (double tap, lock-screen button, impact detection), then saved to your Photos library and the app's own folder. A ride without an incident leaves no file.
- **Your clips are yours.** They stay on your iPhone. If iCloud Photos is turned on, Apple syncs them like any other video — that is your Photos setting, not Dashhound.
- **Calls are never recorded.** During a phone call the clip's sound is replaced by silence.
- **No recognition.** Dashhound never analyses faces, voices or number plates.
- **Glasses data** (video, microphone) goes from your glasses to your iPhone through Meta's toolkit and the Meta AI app, under Meta's terms.
- **"Report a bug"** opens a GitHub page pre-filled with the app version, iPhone model and iOS version. Nothing is sent unless you submit it yourself.

Filming people may be regulated where you live (in France, for example, identifiable people cannot be published without consent). Share clips with those who need them — police, insurer, the other party — not social media.
